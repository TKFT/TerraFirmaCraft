### Changes
- Reworked large-scale terrain generation to mimic plate tectonics
    - Each continent area will now be bordered by convergent or divergent boundaries
    - These may be subduction zones, rift valleys, collisional mountain ranges, and ocean ridges
    - Stratovolcanoes are limited to subduction zones, but other varieties of volcano remain independent of tectonics

- Oceans
    - All ocean biomes made deeper, with trenches extending to y = 0
    - Atolls can now be found in tropical oceans and deep oceans
    - Removed sand-bar style islands from generation
    - Trenches are now found only in subduction zones
    - Mid-Ocean ridges occur in the deep ocean

- Island Chains
    - Volcanic island arcs form in subduction zones
    - Barrier islands/barrier reef chains form on the passive margins of continents

- Karst Biomes
    - Cenotes made larger and can have overhangs
    - Tower karsts reworked to be larger with more diverse shapes

- Volcano Changes
    - New giant stratovolcanoes in a variety of shapes, made of layers of igneous extrusive rocks and tuff
    - Stratovolcanoes replace the old cinder cones in mountain biomes
    - Cinder Cones now occur in volcanic mountain islands, on shield volcanoes, in canyons, and in rift valleys

- Carver Caves
    - Made larger and rarer

- Coastal Terrace Biomes
    - Now generates slightly overhanging cliffs more consistently, instead of occasionally generating perfectly vertically

- Rivers
    - Large rivers now carve wide river valleys through mountains and other high elevation biomes

- General
    - Plains made flatter and lower
    - Rolling hills made hillier
    - Forest soil blending improved
    - Make bamboo features less square

- Mountains (Inland variants only, oceanic mountains unchanged)
    - Generate in more solid ranges (less likely to be interrupted by plateaus etc)
    - Generate more ridges and valleys, and fewer isolated peaks
    - Made substantially taller

- Rocks and Minerals
    - Allow igneous intrusive to be present on the surface of volcanic regions
    - Allow more metamorphic rocks to be present at the surface of uplift regions
    - Allow cinnabar to generate in high-elevation rocks
    - Extend the vertical ranges of several other ores to match new mountain heights
    - Remove deep ruby ore, and move it to collisional mountains
    - Add missing gem ore info to the field guide
